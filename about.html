HISTORIC JOURNEY

Our 25+ Year Journey (2000 – 2026)

From a trusted local village store to a modern, digital-first retail destination.

1

Year 2000: Humble Beginnings by Vinod Kumar Gupta

EST. 2000

Store established near Budhiya Mai Mandir at Tarajeevanpur Chauraha. Initial focus on stainless steel kitchen utensils, kerosene stove repair parts, and honest local customer service.

2

Year 2010: Expansion into Plasticware & Cold Drinks

GROWTH PHASE

Introduced high-grade plastic household items (buckets, tubs, drums) and became a premier local distribution point for chilled beverages and cold drinks (Thums Up, Sprite, Maaza, Coca-Cola).

3

Year 2018: Launch of Commercial Auto Logistics

LOGISTICS EXPANSION

To assist village customers with goods transportation, agricultural produces, and wedding event logistics, commercial loader autos were integrated into the business services.

4

Year 2026: 25+ Years of Legacy & Digital Retail Portal

PRESENT DAY

Celebrated 25+ years of trusted service by launching a full-fledged cloud-enabled digital portal with live product catalogs, WhatsApp ordering, call support, and modern retail management for our customers.
